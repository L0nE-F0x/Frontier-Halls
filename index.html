      What is this?

      One building, ten halls, one clock

      Frontier Halls is a cutaway of ten frontier labs drawn as one building. The halls sit around a court. A clock stands in the middle of it, and every wall clock in the building is reading that one.

      The figures are models. They are not on a path someone laid down: every few seconds each one decides where to go from the hour and from its own habits. Click a figure to read who it is. Click a floor to enter the hall. The row along the bottom names every hall, and 0 frames the whole block.

      Ai2 and Kimi are the first rooms of two quarters the building is growing into, an open-source quarter and an Asian quarter. The unfinished frame on Kimi's outer wall is where the next room attaches. A sidewalk with trees and lamps runs around the outside.

      The picture is drawn by a software rasteriser into a small buffer, reduced to a handful of inks, and scaled up with the pixels left square. Drag to look, scroll to zoom, and turn the building with Q and E. Any panel can be folded away. Press \ when you want the drawing on its own.
